 The Kourend & Kebos Diary is an Achievement Diary whose tasks revolve around areas in Great Kourend as well as the Kebos Lowlands .

 Several skill, quest, and item requirements are needed to complete all tasks. Unless stated otherwise, temporary skill boosts can be used to meet the skill requirements.

 Rewards can be claimed from Elise , south-east of the statue of King Rada I in the courtyard of the Kourend Castle . Tasks may be done in any order, but the previous tiers of a diary must be completed to claim rewards of the next tier.
