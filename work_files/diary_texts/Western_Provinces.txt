 The Western Provinces Diary is an Achievement Diary whose tasks revolve around areas in the western provinces of Gielinor , such as the Tree Gnome Stronghold , Feldip Hills , Tirannwn , and Ape Atoll .

 Several skill, quest, and item requirements are needed to complete all tasks. Unless stated otherwise, temporary skill boosts can be used to meet the skill requirements.

 Rewards can be claimed from the Elder Gnome child just south of the Grand Tree in the Tree Gnome Stronghold . Tasks may be done in any order, but the previous tiers of a diary must be completed to claim rewards of the next tier.
